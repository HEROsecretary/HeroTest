Fall 2026 HERO Application for Non-prior and Prior EMTs

The Hopkins Emergency Response Organization is now accepting applications for individuals interested in joining! Non-prior applicants need no prior medical knowledge or experience. They will be trained during intersession 2027 in a 3-week intensive EMT course with course tuiton covered by HERO! Prior EMTs that currently hold a state EMT-B license, have completed an EMT course, have taken the NREMT exam, and/or have started an EMT course with the intention of recieving their certification elsewhere are also eligible to apply.

Please complete the CORRECT APPLICATION and sign-up for a meet & greet by Sunday, September 27th at 11:59 PM (there will be no extensions to this deadline). Read all associated directions!
